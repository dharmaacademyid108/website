Logo directions

Each is drawn in the site's line language and shown at the sizes that matter — nav, favicon, and reversed on the green banner.

One thing to weigh first: the ring-and-offset-dot is currently documented as the rule every other mark on the site echoes. Replacing it means that echo changes too — not a blocker, but a knock-on to plan for.

0 — Current: ring and offset dot

Reads as a moon with a companion point, or a whole with an individual beside it. Calm and abstract — but it says nothing specific about this school. The same mark would suit almost any contemplative brand.

The closest fit to what you actually teach. One small act sets rings moving outward — karma, and jiri rita: happiness that grows by reaching others. It also answers your own headline, "seperti air yang mengalir". Legible at every size.
