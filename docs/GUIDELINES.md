## 1. What this website is for

- Show we're a real, active Buddhist community — not a cult, not a one-person guru project.
- Show that we are part of an international community based in Japan, and our Dharma Academy ID is the official Indonesian branch of International Buddhism Academy that are based in Toyama, Japan. However is the problem is that International Buddhist Academy is actually Jodo Shinshu Shinrankai which is no problem in Indonesia, however in the past I had encounter people that thinks that we are a cult based on that name. The problem is that if we mention International Buddhist Academy, when people search in google it takes them to a Buddhism page in Nepal which has nothing to do with us. So we are having trouble affiliating our Dharma Academy Id with Shinrankai without giving a cult like sensei but also not mislead them to the wrong Internatinal Buddhist Academy, 
- Indonesians are not familiar to Buddhism in general moreover Pureland Buddhism. In that sense what is important to be conveyed through the website is:
Buddhism's spiritual practice are daily practices, anyone can practice it regardless of their background and so on, the spirit of Pureland Buddhism is jiri rita meaning "making others happy is making oneself happy". Therefore our activities are driven and done from gratitude and happiness that occurs naturally from listening to the teachings and practice the teachings such as jiri rita, therefore we cannot help but to share and convey the happiness to others.
- Explain what Pureland Buddhism (Jodo Shinshu) is to Indonesians who've never heard of it, in plain welcoming language.
- Give visitors clear information what sort of activities we have and how to join them through the website in an enjoyable, easy to understand, and seamless way.
- We have four kinds of Activites in Indonesia. The main one is our free online Introductory Buddhism sessions where anyone can learn 8 topics on the basics of Buddhism in an interactive zoom sessions with our official teacher of Buddhism. The other one is Good Karma Lab, this one is a non-online session held usually twice a month in South Denspasar area, where we gather together to learn Buddhism through short stories from the book "Something You Forgot Along the Way" by Kentetsu Takamori our sensei, through reading a story on wisdom and learning we will have discussions, Q&A, and also prepared topic points from our slides, where the participants can discuss and ask questions. The motto of Good Karma Lab is taken directly from the motto in our Buddhism Academy in Toyama, where people are trained to live based on Buddhist principles "daily life = spiritual path" learning basic common sense. The reason why it is called Good karma Lab is to counter the general conception that Karma is synonymous with bad fortune, bad luck, or a sort of being that can avenge our suffering upon others. Rather to convey that karma is the seeds we plan through our actions that decides our life's fate. Good Karma Lab is a Lab, where we learn, analyze, and most importantly learn to practice good karma in our day to day life from waking up to sleeping. 
The third one is to be an official member/student of Dharma Academy ID, where they can continue the zoom sessions all up to 40 topics, attend our zoom group sessions, attend review sessions, listen to lectures with a sensei from Japan, receive learning materials, full access to our members only website, access to our more than 10 animes and a lot of manga, also to attend our main events in Toyama held every May and October. To become a member is to walk in the noble path of dharma, jiri rita. As the subscription payment its not just for them to receive the dharma and other materials, but also to make it possible for others to encounter dharma, and attain happiness. 
- Make clear that anyone can attend and learn — no need to already be Buddhist or have any religious background.

**How much each thing matters:**
- Proving we're legitimate and trustworthy — most important
- Explaining things clearly — equally important
- Feeling welcoming and accessible to beginners — important
- Getting people to actually contact us / join a class — smallest slice, but the end goal

## 3. Homepage — section by section

The homepage needs to answer 3 questions in the first few seconds: *What is this? Is it legit? What can I do here?*

1. **Hero** — Big tagline + short intro sentence + a button to the Lesson page. Background: a calm photo (temple or teaching scene).
2. **Who We Are** — A short few paragraphs about the org, with a photo. Must clearly mention the Shinrankai Japan connection.
3. **Japan Connection banner** — A standalone highlighted section that says we're part of Shinrankai (Japan) with a link to shinrankai.jp. This exists on its own because Indonesian visitors are naturally skeptical of unfamiliar religious groups — so this needs to be obvious, not buried.
4. **What We Teach** — Plain, jargon-free explanation of what Pureland Buddhism actually teaches.
5. **Our Activities** (renamed from "Our Classes") — Four short cards, not two:
   - **Kelas Intro Online** — free, 8 topics, Zoom → links to Lesson page
   - **Good Karma Lab** — in-person, Denpasar, twice a month, open to anyone via advance registration → links to Event page
   - **Jadi Siswa Resmi** (Membership) — 40 topics, monthly contribution → links to Lesson page
   - **Ikut Acara di Jepang** — Toyama, May & October, members-only → links to Event/Student Privilege page
6. **Recent Events** — 2–3 real event photos with names/descriptions, proving the community is active. Link to Event page.
7. **Books** — 2–3 featured books with covers and 1-line descriptions. Link to Book page.
8. **Final CTA** — One closing line inviting people to reach out, with a button to Contact.

## 4. Look and feel

**Vibe we want:** Calm, credible, grounded, welcoming. Feels like a real institution connected to Japan — not a new-age wellness brand, not a corporate charity site.

**Reference points:**
- shinrankai.jp — for the "this is legitimate" feeling
- The Philippines Pureland site — for scope and warm community feeling

**Colors:** Confirmed (2026-07-05) — warm cream background, terracotta accent, deep teal for buttons/banners. Replaces both the earlier brown/gold idea and the pink/purple "Lollipop" placeholder test.

**Fonts:** Confirmed — a single rounded, friendly sans-serif (Nunito) for both headings and body, no separate serif.

**Photos:** Always real photos — real temple, real classes, real students. Never generic stock photos of "a monk" or "Buddhism" imagery.

**Buttons/corners:** Slightly rounded, not sharp, not pill-shaped. Calm hover effects, no flashy animation.

## 7. Ground rules (not up for casual debate, but flag if you disagree)

- Indonesian is the main language for all visitor-facing text; English support is secondary, for credibility/search — no full English version planned yet.
- No online payment on the site for now — bank transfer instructions only.
- No automated event calendar — Event page is just a photo/documentation page.
- No member login/portal on this site — that lives elsewhere (purelandschool.com).
- Every image needs a real, descriptive Indonesian caption/alt text (also helps search ranking).
- Always pair "Shinrankai" with clear context (Toyama, Japan; our sensei) — never lead with "International Buddhist Academy" alone, since that phrase alone leads to an unrelated Nepal search result. Never raise "cult" concerns proactively on the site — let real activity documentation speak for itself.
